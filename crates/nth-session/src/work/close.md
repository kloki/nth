Close a work item with your verdict: done, or failed. Only you close items; a subagent returning one leaves the judgement to you, so read its answer and check the work as far as you need to first.

A cycle's items must all be closed before the next cycle's can be assigned. Instead of closing a returned item you may fix its brief with work_item and assign it again. Closing a pending item drops it: nobody works on it. A running item cannot be closed; stop it with task_stop first, or let it return.
